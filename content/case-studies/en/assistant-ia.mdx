## The problem

<Todo>describe the host company (sector, size — nothing confidential) and the exact need: which questions did the assistant have to handle, for which users?</Todo>

A language model on its own knows nothing about an organisation's internal documents, and happily makes things up when it doesn't know. The point of the internship: build an assistant that answers **from the actual document corpus**, with answers traceable to their sources.

## The constraints

- A living document corpus: answers had to reflect the current state of the documents, not a snapshot frozen into a model.
- <Todo>the internship's real constraints: data confidentiality, API budget, deadlines, existing systems</Todo>

## The decisions

<Decision
  n="01"
  statut="adopted"
  titre="Augment the model with retrieval rather than specialise it"
  contexte="The assistant had to answer from internal documents that keep changing."
  retenu="A retrieval-based approach (RAG, complemented with CAG): for every question, the model receives the relevant excerpts from the corpus. Documents can change without retraining, and every answer can cite its sources."
  ecarte="Fine-tuning a model on the corpus: costly to redo at every document update, and with no traceability from answers back to sources."
